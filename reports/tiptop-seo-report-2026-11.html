Tip Top Hybrid — what we found and what we shipped

Search Console diagnosis, live SERP review, keyword targeting, page rebuilds, schema, and the 90-day off-page plan for tiptophybrid.com.

20 November 2026 Prepared for Tip Top Hybrid tiptophybrid.com

1. Executive summary

The site had a split personality. The blog was doing the heavy lifting in Google, while the pages that sell repairs were almost invisible. Six service pages averaged 220–690 words, two of them ranked for zero commercial queries, and there was no About page.

We rebuilt the service section, the services hub, testimonials and About around real search demand, added service and FAQ schema, and fixed a review-count claim that could not be supported. The off-page profile is weak but the local competitors are weak too — this is a winnable market with citations, reviews and a handful of genuine local links.

Schema added

  • Service + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList on all six service pages.
  • CollectionPage + ItemList + FAQ on the services hub.
  • AggregateRating + Review on testimonials, matching the visible 5.0 / 8 Google reviews.
  • AboutPage + FAQ on About; ContactPage + FAQ on Contact.

Site-wide fixes

  • Added About to the desktop and mobile navigation and the footer quick links.
  • Fixed 72 city pages that claimed “5 stars, 180+ reviews”. The Google Business Profile shows 8 reviews, so the claim was a trust and compliance risk. All now say “5.0 rated on Google”.
  • Reduced Bunny CDN browser caching from 30 days to 10 minutes so updates appear without a hard refresh.
  • Every service page links to the blog posts that already rank, and those posts link back.

5. Off-page plan, 90 days

Fix the foundation

  1. Export the backlink profile, mark directory spam and PBNs, disavow in Search Console. Spam score 60 needs attention.
  2. One NAP everywhere: Tip Top Hybrid · 1894 W 9th St Ste B, Upland, CA 91786 · 909-238-7028.
  3. Confirm www is the only indexed host and the CDN host canonicalises to it.

Google Business Profile — highest return, no cost

  • Primary category Auto repair shop; secondary hybrid battery, auto electrical, battery store.
  • All six services with descriptions; 30+ real photos refreshed monthly; one post per week.
  • Answer every review within 48 hours, mention the service and city.
  • Seed 10 Q&As from the service-page FAQ blocks.

Reviews — target 40 by May 2027

QR card at pickup, same-day text with the Google link, no incentives. Six to eight new reviews a month is enough to pass the local set.

Citations

Yelp, BBB, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Nextdoor, RepairPal, Carfax Service Shop, Mechanic Advisor, Upland and Montclair chambers. Exact NAP, one category, no keyword-stuffed business names.

Six to ten real local links

Upland Chamber, city community pages, Inland Valley Daily Bulletin, local car clubs and hybrid owner groups, school auto programs, parts partners, a local charity sponsorship. No paid guest-post networks.

Link-earning content

  • Inland Empire hybrid battery heat study using anonymised shop data.
  • Prius Gen 3 head gasket cost guide — the most complete in California.
  • Brake actuator code library (C1256, C1310, C1391).
  • “Is it worth fixing?” repair-vs-value calculator.
