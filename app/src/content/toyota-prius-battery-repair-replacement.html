The Importance of Hybrid Battery Maintenance

The high-voltage battery is the heart of your hybrid system. Over time, factors like temperature, charge cycles, and age cause the performance of individual cells to degrade unevenly. This leads to reduced fuel economy, sluggish acceleration, and eventually, dashboard warning lights.

Choosing repair or reconditioning over a costly replacement can save you thousands. When replacement is necessary, we use only OEM or high-quality certified battery packs that meet or exceed manufacturer specifications.
